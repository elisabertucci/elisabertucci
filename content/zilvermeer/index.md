Zilvermeer is a provincial recreation domain in Mol, Belgium, run by APB Provinciaal Recreatiedomein Zilvermeer. For my postgraduate capstone, a cross-cultural team and I worked with them to design new services and spatial concepts for the site — including a new playground.

We started by mapping Zilvermeer's context and its relationship to the Province of Antwerp as an institution, then moved into sketches, light renders, personas and service blueprints to test ideas with the people who'd actually use them.

Capstone project for my postgraduate in Space & Service Design, Thomas More Hogeschool (2025–2026).
