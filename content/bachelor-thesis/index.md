    title: "It started as environmental research, not a design brief — deep in life-cycle assessment and waste-based biomaterials. Before sketching a screen, I benchmarked 6 platforms (Material ConneXion, Materiom, MaterialDistrict, Brain of Materials, Material Bank, Granta EduPack) into one composite user-flow, then ran 10 open-ended interviews across Genoa's design, architecture, and engineering students."
  - src: 3.jpg
    title: The results were telling — outdated library loans, a single YouTuber to trust, LLMs and forum threads, a friend's carpentry shop for the one material the university couldn't offer.
  - src: 4.jpg
    title: The sharpest pattern — an architect and a structural engineer can look at the same material and want opposite things from it, one calculating load, one judging how it reads in a render. That reframed the brief — not "list materials," but one classification serving both, cross-linked into separate Design and Architecture views.
  - src: 5.jpg
    title: A few interviewees had never heard of bio-based materials — until I described them, then they were hooked. So the datasheet doesn't just describe a material, it surfaces similar, possibly more sustainable ones too — an environmental-research thesis quietly nudging every search toward a better option.
