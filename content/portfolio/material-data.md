Material.Data is my bachelor's thesis project: a digital library that catalogues materials — their properties, sustainability profile and real-world applications — for design and engineering teams to work from together.

The idea came out of a problem I kept hitting in studio projects: materials information scattered across supplier PDFs, personal notes and half-remembered workshop sessions, with nothing a whole team could search or compare against.

For it, I researched how design and engineering students and professionals actually look for and compare materials, then structured a classification system that works across both disciplines, and designed the interface for browsing and comparing entries.

Thesis project for my Bachelor's in Product & Communication Design, Università degli Studi di Genova (2021–2024).
